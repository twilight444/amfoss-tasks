## Tell us about a problem you've noticed around you that you would genuinely like to solve. Why does it matter?

A problem I’ve noticed a lot is how misleading tech marketing can be for regular people when buying a smartphone or laptop. Brands always push big numbers and buzzwords, like 200MP cameras, crazy peak brightness, or just relying on brand hype, and people end up spending money on devices that don't actually deliver what they promise. On-paper specs don't really describe the real-life performance. Most people who aren't into tech don't really know how to look into real benchmarks, processor speeds, or heating issues, so they end up buying something that doesn't perform well or last long without even realizing it.

This matters to me because gadgets are expensive, and I hate seeing people waste their hard-earned money just because they don't understand hardware specs or got tricked by marketing. A lot of people also genuinely don't know which device to buy based on their daily usage. I’d genuinely like to work on or build something like a tool or platform that simplifies all these raw benchmarks into an honest, easy-to-understand price-to-performance rating, so anyone can find the right device for their exact needs and know if it is actually worth their money before buying it.
